# AdvocateDesk Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 September 2026

This initial policy explains how AdvocateDesk may handle information entered by a law practice.

## Information handled
AdvocateDesk may process account details, organization details, client and case records, hearing dates, tasks, documents, notes, and finance records entered by authorized users.

## Purpose
Information is used to provide practice-management features, authenticate users, maintain organization workspaces, synchronize records, and provide support.

## Access and sharing
Workspace information should be accessible only to authorized members of the relevant organization. We do not sell workspace information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the service.

## Security
Access controls, authentication, database policies, and secure storage should be used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and deletion
The organization administrator is responsible for deciding how long practice records should be retained. Requests for deletion or account closure should be sent to the service operator.

## Legal and professional responsibility
AdvocateDesk is a software tool and does not provide legal advice. Customers remain responsible for confidentiality, privilege, lawful processing, backups, retention, and compliance with professional obligations.
