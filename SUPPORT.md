## How customers should report an issue

Please include:

1. Account email or organization name.
2. Page/module where the issue occurred.
3. Steps to reproduce the issue.
4. Device and browser.
5. Screenshot or error message, if available.
6. Whether the issue affects one user or the entire organization.

Do not send passwords, secret keys, payment card details, or confidential case documents through ordinary email.

## Severity guidance

- **Critical:** Login unavailable, data loss, unauthorized access, or a security concern.
- **High:** A major module is unusable or records cannot be saved.
- **Normal:** A feature is partially inconvenient but a workaround exists.
- **Low:** Cosmetic issue, suggestion, or documentation correction.

## Support process

1. Acknowledge the request.
2. Confirm the affected account and issue severity.
3. Reproduce the issue without requesting unnecessary confidential data.
4. Record the resolution and any follow-up action.
5. Close the request only after communicating the outcome.

## Security incidents

For suspected unauthorized access, immediately stop sharing sensitive information, preserve relevant timestamps and screenshots, and contact the official support address. The incident-response process must be finalized before commercial launch.
