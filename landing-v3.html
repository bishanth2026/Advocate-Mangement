FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Who is AdvocateDesk for?

Solo advocates, chambers and small law offices that want a structured digital workflow for daily practice management.

What can I manage?

Clients, cases, hearings, calendar activities, documents, tasks, fees, payments and reports.

Can I see a live demonstration?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute demo and we’ll walk through the workflow.

Does it work on mobile?

The interface is responsive for desktop and mobile use. Production multi-device/cloud deployment can be provided as an upgrade.

Does WhatsApp send automatically?

The current workflow uses user-clicked, pre-filled WhatsApp links. Automatic WhatsApp Business API messaging is a separate future integration.
